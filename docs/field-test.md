## How I test
1. Wait for an overhead alert (or a predicted pass inside my threshold).
2. Note the alert time and the "Look ..." instruction.
3. Go outside facing the direction I set in Settings and look where the extension says.
4. Record when the plane actually appeared, how far from the predicted spot, and whether the ETA was early or late.
